Account & Data Deletion

Under Google Play User Data policies, Spendly AI users have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal and financial records.

Important Information Before Deleting

  • All personal profile details, authentication credentials, and preferences will be permanently wiped.
  • All transaction history, expense logs, income entries, categories, and budget data stored on cloud servers will be permanently deleted.
  • This action is irreversible and deleted data cannot be recovered.
  • Account deletion requests are typically processed within 3 to 7 business days.

How to Request Deletion

To request permanent deletion of your Spendly AI account, please use either of the verified methods below and provide the email address registered with your Spendly AI account:

Request via WhatsApp Request via Email

What Happens After Your Request?

Once we verify that you are the legitimate owner of the registered account, we will initiate complete data purging across our Google Firebase authentication and database servers. You will receive a final confirmation message once the deletion is finalized.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 23, 2026 • Spendly AI

Spendly AI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and financial information. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices for the Spendly AI mobile application (Package: com.spendlyai.app).

1. Information We Collect

To provide personal expense tracking and budgeting tools, Spendly AI may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile photo when registering or authenticating via Google Sign-In or email.
  • Financial & Expense Data: Records of transactions, expense amounts, income entries, categories, notes, and budgets entered by you.
  • Audio / Voice Input: Voice commands recorded temporarily if you use speech-to-text to input expenses (voice data is processed for transcription and not stored permanently).
  • Device & Usage Information: Device model, operating system version, anonymous diagnostic identifiers, app crash logs, and performance statistics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, personalize, and synchronize your expense and budget records across your devices.
  • To authenticate users and ensure authorized account access.
  • To deliver reminders and push notifications for budget alerts and periodic summaries.
  • To monitor application stability, detect bugs, and enhance overall performance.

3. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

Spendly AI integrates trusted third-party service providers that process data under industry-standard security protocols:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure user authentication (Firebase Auth), real-time cloud database storage (Cloud Firestore), and push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to deliver non-personalized or personalized advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.
  • Google Play Billing: When purchasing subscriptions or premium features, transactions are handled exclusively by Google Play. We do not store or process your credit card or payment credentials.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical, administrative, and physical safeguards to protect user data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All network communication between the app and backend services is encrypted using HTTPS / TLS protocols.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active. You may request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated transaction records at any time. For instructions on how to request account deletion, please visit our Account Deletion Page.

6. Children's Privacy

Spendly AI is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
